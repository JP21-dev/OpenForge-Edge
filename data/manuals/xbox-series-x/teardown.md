# Before you start

Power down and unplug the console. Use ESD precautions. Tools: T8 Torx driver, spudger, blunt tweezers. iFixit lists a T8 security bit, but notes every screw is standard T8. Keep screws sorted by stage: lengths range from 5.2 mm to 35 mm.

# Back panel

2 screws, T8, 7.4 mm. Both are hidden: one under a small sticker near the base, one under a larger sticker on the back panel. Peel each sticker only as far as needed. Unclip the panel with a spudger at the gaps on the left and right near the base, then lift it up and off; it hooks into a gap at the top of the shell.

# Fan

3 screws, T8: one 10.5 mm pancake-head screw and two 8.8 mm screws, holding the fan to the center chassis. Disconnect the fan cable by its connector, not the wires, then slide the fan out. It only fits one way. On reassembly, route the cable under its guide on the fan housing.

# Optical drive

Lift the locking tab that holds the base to the shell and rotate the base counterclockwise to release it. 2 screws, T8, 8.8 mm, hold the drive's vibration isolator: one on the base and one on top of the isolator. Walk the isolator off; its silicone pads grip the drive. Disconnect the power connector with blunt tweezers (lift at the edges) and the data cable by hand, then pull the drive out. The drive is paired to the motherboard.

# Center chassis out of the shell

Flip up the metal lock on the USB ribbon cable (fragile) and pull its black tab; it may be glued and need gentle heat. On the power button cable, press the metal tab on the board connector before pulling its tab. 3 screws, T8, 7.4 mm, hold the center chassis to the shell. Peel the taped USB ribbon off the heatsink, then slide the chassis toward the fan grille and lift it out without pinching either ribbon.

# Power port

3 screws, T8: two 13.1 mm and one 35 mm. Lift the power connector out of its recess, open the lid on the plastic cable guide and de-route the cable.

# Corner cover and antenna board

Corner cover: 1 screw, T8, 8.8 mm. Some units have no corner cover. Antenna board: 3 screws, T8, 9.6 mm; pull the board straight off its connector.

# Board shield

9 screws, T8: six black 8.8 mm, two silver 35 mm and one silver 13.1 mm.

# Chassis strap and power supply

Unlatch the chassis strap from the tabs on each side of the PSU. Remove the three silver 35 mm screws from the PSU and leave the fourth, black screw in place.

# Cables and lifting the chassis

Disconnect the 10-pin motherboard power connector: squeeze its lock and lift straight up. Disconnect the interconnect cable: some units have locking tabs on both sides, others a single tab in the middle pressed with a fingernail; pull straight out. Lift the center chassis off the boards by its edges, not by the PSU, and feed the interconnect cable through its cutout.

# SSD

Pry off the SSD shield with a spudger, keeping its thermal pads intact on both sides. 1 screw, T8, 5.2 mm. The drive pops up at a shallow angle; pull it out of the M.2 connector.

# Screw totals by stage

Back panel 2 × 7.4 mm. Fan 1 × 10.5 mm and 2 × 8.8 mm. Optical drive 2 × 8.8 mm. Center chassis 3 × 7.4 mm. Power port 2 × 13.1 mm and 1 × 35 mm. Corner cover 1 × 8.8 mm. Antenna board 3 × 9.6 mm. Board shield 6 × 8.8 mm black, 2 × 35 mm silver, 1 × 13.1 mm silver. PSU 3 × 35 mm silver. SSD 1 × 5.2 mm.
