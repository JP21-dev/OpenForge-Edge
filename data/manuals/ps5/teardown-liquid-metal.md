# Screws and drivers

Stand: 1 screw, 26.5 mm long. Driver: coin or flathead.

Fan shroud: 4 screws in total (2 × 23.3 mm, 1 × 11.4 mm, 1 × 31 mm). Driver: TR9 Torx Security.

SSD cover: 1 screw, 17 mm long. Driver: Phillips.

Case: 11 screws in total (6 × 18.6 mm, 2 × 23.3 mm, 2 × 43.2 mm, 1 × 7.3 mm). Driver: TR8 Torx Security.

Top shield plate: 42 screws in total (41 × 7.3 mm, 1 × 43.2 mm). Driver: TR8 Torx Security.

Main board: 5 screws in total. 1 × 11 mm SSD screw and 2 × 11.8 mm APU tension bracket screws use a Phillips driver; 2 × 7.3 mm board screws use TR8 Torx Security.

Keep screws sorted by stage: lengths differ and a long screw in a short hole can damage the board.

# Disassembly order

1. Remove the stand screw, stands and cubby cap, then the right faceplate (clips) and grille.
2. Remove the fan shroud (TR9), disconnect and lift the fan. Pry only on the wire cover, never the fan wires.
3. Remove the SSD cover and disconnect the optical drive cable. Pull cables by their connectors.
4. Remove the 11 case screws (TR8), the optical drive ribbon and the optical drive.
5. Disconnect ribbon and antenna cables (some revisions have no antenna wires).
6. Remove the 42 top shield screws and the shield. Replacement thermal pads must be the same thickness as the originals.
7. Disconnect the USB board cable.
8. Loosen the two APU tension bracket screws alternately, a little at a time. Do not remove or install one screw fully before the other.
9. Separate the main board from the power supply prongs; it is held by thermal pads. Do not press the board onto the work surface.

# Re-applying liquid metal

1. Wear gloves. Clean the heatsink contact surface with isopropyl alcohol above 90 % until completely clean.
2. Clean the APU die, leaving the foam barrier in place. Keep liquid metal off the board: it is conductive and will short components.
3. Apply a drop about the size of a small matchstick head to the heatsink and spread it.
4. Spread a thin layer on the APU with a cotton swab.

Another community source quotes about 1 g of liquid metal; use only enough for a thin, complete layer.
